# Image Pad

Paste an image, touch it up, take it back out.

## In

Paste an image from the clipboard.

## Edit

- Rotate 90°, each direction
- Flip horizontal / vertical
- Crop
- Resize

## Out

- Export as `.png` or `.jpg`
- Copy to clipboard

## Open questions

- Crop interaction: drag handles or numeric box.
- JPEG export quality.
- How much history (no undo, or a small undo stack of operations).
